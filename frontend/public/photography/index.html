Tnega

Photography portfolio

Saba Azadegan, 2017 to 2025

I studied photography for about two years, first in the photography course at the University of Tehran and then on my own. The Saadabad, Tehran, Nature vs human, Darband and Tajrish, Lasem and Damavand, Isfahan, Mountain road, Still life and detail, Light painting and Unseen series and the 2017 cat photographs were taken on a Nikon D810 in Iran; the rest on a phone, in Lisbon and on the coast at Cascais. The last two albums are not photography. AI-generated (ComfyUI) holds images I made with ComfyUI from my own photographs, and Digital art holds characters, logos and images I made with AI tools for my own projects.

AI-generated (ComfyUI)

24 works, 2026

Not photographs. I made these with ComfyUI, a free, open-source program that runs image models on your own computer as a chain of connected steps. For each image I chose two of my photographs on this page that share an idea, blended them into one starting picture, and ran it on my laptop through an image-to-image step with the open DreamShaper 8 LCM model, which repaints the blend in eight steps, guided by a one-line description.

Strength sets how far the model may move away from my photographs: 0.55 stays close, 0.70 gives it more freedom. Each pair was made at both strengths with two different seeds. Under each image are the idea behind it and the two photographs it was made from.
