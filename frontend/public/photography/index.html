Saba Azadegan, 2017 to 2025

I studied photography for about two years, first in the photography course at the University of Tehran and then on my own. The Saadabad, Tehran, Lasem and Damavand, and Still life and detail series and the 2017 cat photographs were taken on a Nikon D810 in Iran; the rest on a phone, in Lisbon and on the coast at Cascais.
