Photography portfolio

Saba Azadegan, 2017 to 2025

I studied photography for about two years, first in the photography course at the University of Tehran and then on my own. The Saadabad, Tehran, Nature vs human, Darband and Tajrish, Lasem and Damavand, Still life and detail, and Light painting series and the 2017 cat photographs were taken on a Nikon D810 in Iran; the rest on a phone, in Lisbon and on the coast at Cascais.
