Gestures

  • Pinch and moveDraw with light (thumb on index finger)
  • Close a loopSnaps to a circle, triangle, box or star
  • Draw a straight lineBecomes an arrow, pointing where you ended
  • Hold a peace signTake a picture of the sketch
  • Wave an open handWipe light away
  • Hold a fistClear everything
  • Pinch both handsStretch a string, then flick it to play
  • Hold a fingertipPress a button in the bar above

Havaهوا

Explain an idea by drawing it in the air. Sketch boxes, circles and arrows with your fingers while you talk, then hold up a peace sign to keep a picture of it.

On a video call, share this browser tab and the others see your sketch over your face. Hand tracking runs in this browser with Google's MediaPipe; the camera picture stays on this computer and is never uploaded. Works best in Chrome, with your hands about an arm's length from the camera.
